# Tabbit

- Fork: [Blockchains/Tabbit](https://github.com/Blockchains/Tabbit)
- Original: [eftrotto/Tabbit](https://github.com/eftrotto/Tabbit)
- Description: Meal benefits on Solana. Employers fund an on-chain vault in USDC and set each worker's limits; workers sign in with Google and pay by QR with no crypto and no gas; restaurants get paid in seconds at 0.8%, instead of up to 3.6% and 15 days. Every rule is enforced by the program and every payment is public.
