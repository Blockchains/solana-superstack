- Description: Manifest is a public checking layer for records of Solana activity. Trace issues each record and anchors its fingerprint on Solana. Anyone can then check it, no login or wallet: the checker re-reads the transactions, rechecks the reported figures and calculations, and flags any altered claim. An authentic record isn’t always an accurate one.
