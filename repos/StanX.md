# StanX

- Fork: [Blockchains/StanX](https://github.com/Blockchains/StanX)
- Original: [yashop7/StanX](https://github.com/yashop7/StanX)
- Description: A central limit order book(CLOB) prediction market on Solana. Users trade YES/NO outcome tokens against each other at their own prices, orders match peer-to-peer on-chain, and the live orderbook streams to every connected client in real time via a diff-based WebSocket protocol backed by Rust, Redis, and PostgreSQL.
