# PASS

- Fork: [Blockchains/PASS](https://github.com/Blockchains/PASS)
- Original: [codewithpradeep14/PASS](https://github.com/codewithpradeep14/PASS)
- Description: PASS is "money with instructions" on Solana.  Instead of sending unrestricted crypto, you lock funds into a purpose-aware smart escrow with strict rules: category-only spending, per-tx limits, and live countdown timers. Payments are auto-blocked if rules are violated, and unspent funds remain 100% revocable by the owner at any time.
