- Description: Autonomint is a subsidized insurance layer for DeFi. Liquidity providers deposit USDC into an underwriting vault, vault capital can be routed into lending providers such as Kamino or Marginfi, and generated lending yield subsidizes put-option premiums for users buying downside protection.
