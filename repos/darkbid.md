# darkbid

- Fork: [Blockchains/darkbid](https://github.com/Blockchains/darkbid)
- Original: [Amaresh-Saravanan/darkbid](https://github.com/Amaresh-Saravanan/darkbid)
- Description: DarkBid is a decentralized auction platform that leverages Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) and the Solana blockchain to enable privacy-preserving, sealed-bid auctions. This ensures that bids remain secret until the reveal phase, preventing front-running and ensuring a truly fair price discovery mechanism.
