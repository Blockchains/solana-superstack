# SLIP

- Fork: [Blockchains/SLIP](https://github.com/Blockchains/SLIP)
- Original: [vellan-3/SLIP](https://github.com/vellan-3/SLIP)
- Description: SLIP is a Solana wallet trade analyzer. A user pastes their wallet address, the app pulls their recent swaps from chain, prices each entry and exit using historical OHLCV data, and outputs a ranked breakdown of their worst trades with specific, actionable diagnoses. Every result is shareable as a card on X.
