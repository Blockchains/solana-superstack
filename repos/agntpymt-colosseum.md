- Description: AgntPymt is a self-hosted payment gateway for enterprise AI agents. The agent decides what to buy; AgntPymt decides whether it's allowed. It enforces spend caps, human approval above a threshold and a kill switch before any payment is signed, then settles in stablecoins over x402 with a full audit trail. Any agent connects via MCP.
