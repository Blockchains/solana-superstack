- Original: [relfnot/aegis-protocol](https://github.com/relfnot/aegis-protocol)
- Description: Escrow payments for Solana teams, with a shared outflow budget and guardian emergency pause.  Aegis is a Rust/Anchor prototype for teams paying contributors or service providers in scheduled tranches. Each tranche is a separate escrow. Funds are held in an SPL Token vault, and both payments and refunds consume the same per-window budget.
