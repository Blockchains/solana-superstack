# capture

- Fork: [Blockchains/capture](https://github.com/Blockchains/capture)
- Original: [chunli0x/capture](https://github.com/chunli0x/capture)
- Description: Capture is a decentralized Reinforcement Learning Human Feedback (RLHF) platform. Contributors get paid for evaluating AI decisions - Step 1 stake a USDC bond to prove human, Step 2 judge a vision model's outputs (Choose A or B), and Step 3 get paid in USDC when their answer forms a consensus with other contributors.
