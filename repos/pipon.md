# pipon

- Fork: [Blockchains/pipon](https://github.com/Blockchains/pipon)
- Original: [VJyou/pipon](https://github.com/VJyou/pipon)
- Description: p!pon: zero-knowledge credentials from the IC chip in Japan's My Number Card. Prove a fact (age 20+, prefecture, one per person) without handing over your ID. The phone reads the card and proves on device; a browser or a Solana program verifies. Open source, built to be adapted to other national ID chips.
