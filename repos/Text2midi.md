- Description: Text2midi is the first end-to-end model for generating MIDI files from textual descriptions. By leveraging pretrained large language models and a powerful autoregressive transformer decoder, text2midi allows users to create symbolic music that aligns with detailed textual prompts, including musical attributes like chords, tempo, and style.
